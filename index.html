What is an image placeholder? And how to use it

Saturday 30th May 2026

An image placeholder is a temporary image used in web design and development to reserve space for content that will be added later. Placeholders help designers visualize layout, spacing, and structure before final images are available. Common examples include services like Lorem Picsum, Placeholder.com, or static images that represent the dimensions and aspect ratio of the intended final image. To use a placeholder, simply insert the placeholder image URL in place of your final image source, ensuring the dimensions match your planned design.
